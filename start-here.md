## 🕐 I Have 5 Minutes

**Send Email to Your MP:** [Humanti Project Email Templates](https://humantiproject.org/email-templates/) (Canadian-focused, updated October 2025), [CJPME: Phone Your MP for Ceasefire](https://www.cjpme.org/phone_for_ceasefire) (call tool with talking points), [Find Your MP](https://www.ourcommons.ca/members/en).

Template: "Subject: Constituent Calling for [Specific Action]. Dear [MP Name], I am your constituent in [Riding Name], writing to urge immediate action on [specific issue]. [Personal connection - 1-2 sentences]. [Clear ask - what you want them to do]. [2-3 supporting facts]. I look forward to your response on this urgent matter. Sincerely, [Your Name], [Your Address/Postal Code]"

Canadian: [@cjpme](https://www.instagram.com/cjpme/), Coalition of Canadian Palestinian Organizations (local chapters across 13 cities). [Full list →](/people-to-follow)

## ⏰ I Have 1 Hour

**Join or Start Campus Group:** Find existing: SPHR (Canadian campus network, established 1999), SJP (350+ chapters across US/Canada), Canadian Federation of Students, [National SJP](https://www.nationalsjp.org/). No group? Start one: find 3-5 committed co-founders, register as official student organization, develop mission statement, create sustainable structure, build faculty/staff relationships. [Full guide →](/campus-organizing)

**Take on Weekly Action:** Tabling (2-hour shifts, reserve space through student union, create info pamphlets/QR codes, sign-up sheet, 2-3 people per shift—[toolkit →](/campus-organizing/weekly-actions#tabling)). Weekly Vigils (choose symbolic location, obtain permits, 30 min-2 hours, LED candles, signs, readings—[guide →](/campus-organizing/weekly-actions#vigils)). Social Media (manage accounts 1-2 hours daily, weekly content calendar, educational + action posts—[toolkit →](/campus-organizing/weekly-actions#social-media)).

**Attend Local Meetings:** Calgary—[Justice for Palestine Calgary](https://www.instagram.com/jfpcalgary), [Calgary Palestinian Council](https://calgarypalestiniancouncil.ca/). Find Near You—[Coalition of Canadian Palestinian Organizations](https://www.canadapalestine.ca/) (25 orgs across 13 cities), [Canadian BDS Coalition](https://bdscoalition.ca/), search Facebook/Instagram for "[Your City] Palestine solidarity".

**Participate in BDS:** [BDS Movement: What to Boycott](https://bdsmovement.net/get-involved/what-to-boycott), [Canadian BDS Coalition: List of Shame](https://bdscoalition.ca/boycott-list-of-shame/). Key Targets: HP, Puma, SodaStream, Ahava, Barclays, Elbit Systems. [Full BDS resources →](/bds)

## 📣 I Want to Organize

**Lead Divestment Campaign:** Economic pressure on companies profiting from genocide. Educational component forces institutional reckoning. Builds organizing skills and coalition power. Creates concrete, winnable goals. Recent Canadian Successes: York University Faculty Association passed divestment motion, University of Ottawa professors called for divestment, Montreal student strikes (60 associations, 46,000+ students), UBC DIVEST compiled list of 30 corporations.

**4-Phase Process:** Phase 1 - Research (2-3 months): investigate university investments, file Freedom of Information requests, identify complicit companies, document connections ([research toolkit →](/campus-organizing/campaigns#divestment)). Phase 2 - Coalition Building (ongoing): build core team (10-15 committed), engage faculty unions, partner with student groups, connect with community orgs ([coalition guide →](/campus-organizing/campaigns#divestment)). Phase 3 - Public Launch (1-2 months): launch petition (goal: 1000+ signatures), educational campaign, meet with administration, media coverage ([launch guide →](/campus-organizing/campaigns#divestment)). Phase 4 - Escalation (as needed): public demonstrations, coordinated walkouts, encampments (last resort, high risk—[escalation tactics →](/campus-organizing/campaigns#divestment)).

**⚠️ Know Your Rights:** Before major actions, understand risks. [Palestine Legal](https://palestinelegal.org/) provides Know Your Rights workshops. [Complete guide →](/campus-organizing/campaigns#divestment)

**Organize Educational Events:** Film Screenings—select film (check licensing), book room with AV equipment, invite speaker/facilitator, prepare discussion questions ([film screening toolkit →](/campus-organizing/weekly-actions#film-screenings)). Recommended: "Five Broken Cameras" (nonviolent resistance in Bil'in), "The Wanted 18" (animated documentary), "1948: Creation & Catastrophe" (historical context). Speaker Events—Palestinian academics and activists, international law scholars, Canadian MPs/policy experts ([bringing speakers guide →](/campus-organizing/campaigns#speakers)).

**Build Coalitions:** Strategic Partners—climate justice groups (environmental impact of occupation), Indigenous solidarity (colonial frameworks), anti-racism organizations, labor movements, LGBTQ+ groups. Coalition Building—attend partner meetings (1-2 weekly), co-sponsor events, joint social media campaigns, shared resources ([coalition toolkit →](/campus-organizing/campaigns#coalition-building)).

**Take on Leadership Role:** Communications Coordinator (manage social media 1-2 hours daily, write press releases, design graphics), Outreach Coordinator (build coalitions, recruit new members, faculty/staff relationships), Research Coordinator (track university investments, create educational resources, fact-check claims), Events Coordinator (plan speakers/vigils/actions, handle logistics, coordinate volunteers), Finance Coordinator (track budget, plan fundraisers, apply for funding). [All role templates →](/campus-organizing/roles)
