## 🕐 I Have 5 Minutes

**Quick, impactful actions you can take right now:**

### Send a Pre-Written Email to Your MP
Use these ready-to-customize templates to contact your Member of Parliament about Canadian foreign policy:

- **[Humanti Project Email Templates](https://humantiproject.org/email-templates/)** - Canadian-focused, updated October 2025
- **[CJPME: Phone Your MP for Ceasefire](https://www.cjpme.org/phone_for_ceasefire)** - Call tool with talking points
- **[Find Your MP's Contact Info](https://www.ourcommons.ca/members/en)** - House of Commons directory

**Template Structure:**
```
Subject: Constituent Calling for [Specific Action]

Dear [MP Name],

I am your constituent in [Riding Name], writing to urge immediate action on [specific issue].

[Personal connection - 1-2 sentences]
[Clear ask - what you want them to do]
[2-3 supporting facts]

I look forward to your response on this urgent matter.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[Your Address/Postal Code]
```

## 📣 I Want to Organize

**Deep organizing - major time commitment:**

### Lead a Divestment Campaign

**Why Divestment?**
- Economic pressure on companies profiting from occupation
- Educational component forces institutional reckoning
- Builds organizing skills and coalition power
- Creates concrete, winnable goals

**Recent Canadian Successes:**
- York University Faculty Association passed divestment motion
- University of Ottawa professors called for divestment
- Montreal student strikes: 60 associations, 46,000+ students
- UBC DIVEST compiled list of 30 corporations

**The 4-Phase Process:**

**Phase 1: Research (2-3 months)**
- Investigate university investments
- File Freedom of Information requests
- Identify problematic companies
- Document connections
- [Research toolkit →](/campus-organizing/divestment/research)

**Phase 2: Coalition Building (Ongoing)**
- Build core team (10-15 committed)
- Engage faculty unions
- Partner with other student groups
- Connect with community orgs
- [Coalition building guide →](/campus-organizing/divestment/coalitions)

**Phase 3: Public Launch (1-2 months)**
- Launch petition (goal: 1000+ signatures)
- Educational campaign
- Meet with administration
- Media coverage
- [Launch guide →](/campus-organizing/divestment/launch)

**Phase 4: Escalation (As needed)**
- Public demonstrations
- Coordinated walkouts
- Encampments (last resort, high risk)
- [Escalation tactics →](/campus-organizing/divestment/escalation)

**⚠️ Know Your Rights:** Before major actions, understand risks. [Palestine Legal](https://palestinelegal.org/) provides Know Your Rights workshops.

[Complete divestment campaign guide →](/campus-organizing/divestment)
