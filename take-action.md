# 📢 Take Action Hub

## 🎯 Quick Actions (5-10 minutes)

### ✉️ Email Your MP

**Why:** Your voice as a constituent matters.

**What to do:**
- Use [CJPME email templates](https://www.cjpme.org/campaigns)
- Or [Humanti Project templates](https://humantiproject.org/email-templates/)
- Find your MP: [ourcommons.ca/members](https://www.ourcommons.ca/members/en)

**Topics:**
- Ceasefire demands
- Arms embargo
- Humanitarian aid access
- Palestinian rights

**Time:** 5 minutes

---

### 📞 Call Your MP

**Why:** Calls carry more weight than emails.

**What to say:**
> "Hi, I'm [Name], a constituent in [Riding]. I'm calling to urge [MP Name] to call for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza. This matters to me because [personal reason]. Can you pass this message along?"

**CJPME Call Tool:** [cjpme.org/phone_for_ceasefire](https://www.cjpme.org/phone_for_ceasefire) - Provides talking points while you call

**Time:** 5 minutes

---

### 🛍️ Join BDS

**What is BDS?**
Palestinian-led Boycott, Divestment, Sanctions movement.

**Consumer boycott targets:**
- **Sabra & Obela** (hummus brands)
- **Puma** (sponsors Israeli Football Association in settlements)
- **AXA** (invests in Israeli banks funding settlements)
- **Israeli produce** (look for country of origin labels)

**Full list:** [bdsmovement.net](https://bdsmovement.net/)

**Learn more:** [Canadian BDS Coalition](https://bdscoalition.ca/)

**Time:** 5 minutes to learn, ongoing to practice
