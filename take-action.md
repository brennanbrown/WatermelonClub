# 📢 Take Action

Pick one action. Do it today.

---

## 🎯 Quick Actions (5-10 minutes)

**Email Your MP:** Your voice as a constituent matters. Use [CJPME email templates](https://www.cjpme.org/campaigns) or [Humanti Project templates](https://humantiproject.org/email-templates/). Find your MP: [ourcommons.ca/members](https://www.ourcommons.ca/members/en). Topics: ceasefire demands, arms embargo, humanitarian aid access, Palestinian rights. 5 minutes.

**Call Your MP:** Calls carry more weight than emails. Say: "Hi, I'm [Name], a constituent in [Riding]. I'm calling to urge [MP Name] to call for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza. This matters to me because [personal reason]. Can you pass this message along?" [CJPME Call Tool](https://www.cjpme.org/phone_for_ceasefire) provides talking points while you call. 5 minutes.

**Donate:** Canadian organizations (tax receipts): [Islamic Relief Canada](https://www.islamicreliefcanada.org/emergencies/palestine/), [UNRWA](https://www.unrwa.org/), [Palestine Children's Relief Fund](https://www.pcrf.net/). Advocacy organizations: [CJPME Foundation](https://www.cjpmefoundation.org/donate_earmark_humanitarian_2023), [Canadian BDS Coalition](https://bdscoalition.ca/). Alternative: eSIMs for Gaza ([esimsforgaza.com](https://esimsforgaza.com/)). 5 minutes.

**Join BDS:** Palestinian-led Boycott, Divestment, Sanctions movement. Consumer boycott targets: Sabra & Obela (hummus brands), Puma (sponsors Israeli Football Association in settlements), AXA (invests in Israeli banks funding settlements), Israeli produce (look for country of origin labels). [Full list](https://bdsmovement.net/), [Canadian BDS Coalition](https://bdscoalition.ca/). 5 minutes to learn, ongoing to practice.

## 📱 Digital Actions (10-15 minutes)

**Amplify Palestinian Voices:** Follow [Palestinian journalists & activists](/people-to-follow). Share their content. Engage (likes, comments, shares). Use hashtags: #FreePalestine #GazaUnderAttack. Counters social media suppression.

**Sign Petitions:** [CJPME Campaigns](https://www.cjpme.org/campaigns), [Avaaz Palestine](https://www.avaaz.org), check [Watermelon Club](https://berryhouse.ca) for latest (Instagram coming soon). Tip: add a personal comment for more impact.

**Challenge Media Bias:** Report biased coverage ([CJPME Media Project](https://www.cjpmemap.ca/)), write letters to editor, challenge on social media. [Media Literacy Guide](/education/media-literacy)

## 📅 Make It Ongoing

**Weekly:** Share Palestinian content, check for new petitions, attend vigil/tabling.

**Monthly:** Donate to humanitarian org, attend organizing meeting, write to MP or media.

**Long-term:** Join campus group, build campaigns, form coalitions.

## 🎯 By Time Available

**5 Minutes:** Email your MP (use template), donate, sign petition, share Palestinian content.

**1 Hour:** Call your MP (more impact), write personal email (not template), follow 10 Palestinian accounts, learn about BDS.

**Ongoing Time:** [Join campus organizing](/campus-organizing), start weekly tabling, organize film screening, launch divestment campaign.
