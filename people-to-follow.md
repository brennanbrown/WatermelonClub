## 📖 How to Amplify These Voices

### On Social Media

**Follow:**
- Follow accounts across multiple platforms
- Turn on post notifications for key accounts

**Engage:**
- Like, comment, share posts
- Add your own commentary when sharing
- Use relevant hashtags: #FreePalestine #GazaUnderAttack #CdnPoli

**Protect:**
- Report when accounts are suppressed or shadowbanned
- Screenshot important content (in case deleted)
- Don't share graphic content without content warnings

**Support:**
- Donate to organizations if able
- Attend events they organize
- Participate in campaigns they launch

---

### Beyond Social Media

**Cite in Your Work:**
- Use as sources for papers, presentations
- Quote Palestinian voices directly
- Credit properly

**Invite to Campus:**
- Bring speakers to your university
- Host screenings of their work
- Organize Q&As

**Defend:**
- Push back when Palestinian voices are censored
- Challenge biased coverage
- Support when they face attacks

---

## 🚨 Note on Censorship

**Social media platforms routinely suppress Palestinian content:**
- Shadowbanning (limiting reach)
- Account suspensions
- Content removal
- Delayed posts during critical moments

**How to Counter:**
- Engage heavily with Palestinian content
- Report censorship when you see it
- Use multiple platforms
- Archive important content
- Support platform alternatives
