# 🇵🇸 We are Students for Palestine

## 📝 About This Platform

> **A comprehensive platform for Canadian students (ages 16-24) to learn about Palestine, take action, and organize for justice.**

This resource hub was originally created by students in Mohkínstsis (Calgary) and is now maintained by Berry House, none of whom are Palestinian, Arab, or Muslim themselves. We have made a concerted effort to highlight and amplify the voices, perspectives, goals, and calls to action of those who are.

**If any information or resources exist in contradiction to Palestinian calls to action,** please email us at [mail@berryhouse.ca](mailto:mail@berryhouse.ca) or message us on Instagram at [@watermelonclub](https://www.instagram.com/watermelonclub/).

**Important distinction:** Watermelon Club acknowledges the critical difference between **antisemitism** and **anti-Zionism**. We do NOT support antisemitism in any way and encourage reporting any instances of antisemitism (online or in person). **Criticism of Israeli government policies is not antisemitism—it is legitimate political speech grounded in human rights and international law.**

Many Jewish students and allies are central to the Palestinian solidarity movement, including organizations like [Jewish Voice for Peace](https://jewishvoiceforpeace.org/) and [Independent Jewish Voices Canada](https://www.ijvcanada.org/).

## 📊 Why This Matters

### The Situation in Palestine

**Occupation & Apartheid:**
- West Bank under Israeli military occupation since 1967
- Gaza under blockade since 2007 (described as "open-air prison")
- Multiple human rights orgs (HRW, Amnesty, B'Tselem) document apartheid

**Humanitarian Crisis:**
- 5+ million Palestinians in West Bank/Gaza
- 5+ million Palestinian refugees worldwide
- Severe restrictions on movement, water, electricity
- Ongoing violence and displacement

**International Law:**
- Occupation is illegal under international law
- Settlements violate Geneva Conventions
- Palestinians have right to self-determination

**Canadian Context:**
- Canada recognized State of Palestine (Sept 21, 2025)
- Canadian companies and pension funds invested in complicit corporations
- Student movements demanding divestment across Canada

## 📢 About This Site

### Who We Are

This resource hub was created by student organizers in Mohkínstsis (Calgary) and is now maintained by Berry House. We are none of us Palestinian, Arab, or Muslim ourselves, and we have made every effort to center and amplify Palestinian voices.

**Our Commitment:**
- Center Palestinian voices and leadership
- Provide actionable, practical resources
- Focus on Canadian context
- Regular updates and maintenance
- Accessibility for all learners

### Our Principles

**We oppose all forms of bigotry, including antisemitism.**
We make a clear distinction between:
- **Antisemitism:** Hatred or discrimination against Jewish people (OPPOSE)
- **Anti-Zionism:** Political opposition to Zionism and Israeli policies (SUPPORT)

Criticism of Israeli government policy is not antisemitism. Many Jewish students and allies are part of the Palestinian solidarity movement.

**Human rights are universal and non-negotiable.**
