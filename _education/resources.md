## 🎬 Documentaries & Films

### Essential Viewing

**Historical Context:**
- **[Al-Nakba](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLzGHKb8i9vTwtfuXxbuv-rr1z8iUOLuE-)** (2008, 4 parts) - 1948 from Palestinian perspective, presents understanding of events that displaced hundreds of thousands
- **[1948: Creation & Catastrophe](https://www.pbs.org/)** (2017) - Parallel narratives of Israeli and Palestinian experiences
- **[The Price of Oslo](https://www.aljazeera.com/program/al-jazeera-world/2013/9/18/the-price-of-oslo)** - Two-part documentary tracing the secret road to the Oslo Accords

**Life Under Occupation:**
- **[Five Broken Cameras](https://www.pbs.org/pov/films/5brokencameras/)** (2011) - Oscar-nominated chronicle of non-violent resistance in Bil'in village through eyes of Palestinian farmer Emad Burnat
- **[Occupation 101](https://occupation101.tv/)** (2006) - Explores root causes of the Israeli-Palestinian conflict with historical context
- **[Budrus](https://justvision.org/budrus/watch)** - Inspiring documentary about community organizer Ayed Morrar uniting Palestinian factions and Israeli supporters in unarmed movement

**Personal Stories:**
- **[Little Palestine: Diary of a Siege](https://www.theguardian.com/film/2023/oct/26/little-palestine-diary-of-a-siege-review-refugee-camp-yarmouk-syria)** (2021) - Harrowing documentary following life among starving Palestinians in Yarmouk camp during Syria's civil war
- **[Gaza Mon Amour](https://tiff.net/events/gaza-mon-amour)** (2020) - Romantic drama set in Gaza, focusing on life of 60-year-old fisherman
- **[Speed Sisters](https://speedsisters.tv/)** (2015) - First all-female racing team in Middle East, breaking barriers in Palestine

**Israeli Perspectives / Critiques:**
- **[The Gatekeepers](https://www.documentary.org/online-feature/secret-sharers-gatekeepers-exposes-hidden-history)** (2012) - Interviews with six former heads of Shin Bet (Israeli security), providing insights into operations and ethical dilemmas
- **[Foxtrot](https://www.sonyclassics.com/foxtrot/)** (2017 Drama) - Family tragedy that critiques Israeli military establishment

## 📖 Books & Reading Lists

### Essential Reads - History

- **[The Hundred Years' War on Palestine: A History of Settler Colonialism and Resistance, 1917-2017](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781627798556/thehundredyearswaronpalestine)** by Rashid Khalidi - Comprehensive history of Palestinian struggle, accessible through personal lens
- **[The Ethnic Cleansing of Palestine](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Ethnic-Cleansing-of-Palestine/Ilan-Pappe/9781851685554)** by Ilan Pappé - Detailed account of systematic expulsion of Palestinians in 1948
- **[Palestine: A Four Thousand Year History](https://www.bloomsbury.com/ca/palestine-9780755649426/)** by Nur Masalha - Long view of Palestinian identity, challenging common narratives
- **[The Question of Palestine](https://typebooks.ca/products/9780679739883)** by Edward W. Said - Seminal analysis of historical and political complexities
- **[Righteous Victims: A History of the Zionist-Arab Conflict, 1881-2001](https://www.palestine-studies.org/en/node/41025)** by Benny Morris - Balanced perspective on historical conflict

### International Law

- **[The Case for Palestine: An International Law Perspective](https://www.dukeupress.edu/the-case-for-palestine)** by John Quigley - Exploration of legal aspects, advocating for Palestinian rights
- **[Justice for Some: Law and the Question of Palestine](https://www.versobooks.com/)** by Noura Erakat - Essential legal analysis

### Personal Narratives & Literature

- **[Gaza Writes Back](https://justworldbooks.com/books-by-title/gaza-writes-back/)** edited by Refaat Alareer - Short stories by young writers from Gaza
- **[After the Last Sky](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/after-the-last-sky/9780231114493)** by Edward Said - Essays with photos exploring Palestinian history and struggle
- **[The Way to the Spring: Life and Death in Palestine](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/315663/the-way-to-the-spring-by-ben-ehrenreich/)** by Ben Ehrenreich - Detailed narrative of life in occupied territories
- **[My Happiness Bears No Relation to Happiness](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300164275/my-happiness-bears-no-relation-to-happiness/)** by Adina Hoffman - Biography of Palestinian poet Taha Muhammad Ali
- **[From Coexistence to Conquest](https://www.amazon.com/Coexistence-Conquest-International-Arab-Israeli-1891-1949/dp/0745325785)** by Victor Kattan - History of Israeli occupation and territorial expansion

### Graphic Novels

- **[Footnotes in Gaza](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9780805092776/footnotesingaza)** by Joe Sacco - Investigative journalism documenting 1956 massacres
- **[Palestine](https://www.fantagraphics.com/products/palestine)** by Joe Sacco - Classic graphic journalism

### Poetry

- **[Mural](https://www.versobooks.com/en-ca/products/2101-mural)** by Mahmoud Darwish - Poignant collection on exile, identity, resistance
- Works by Rafeef Ziadah, Remi Kanazi

### Identity & Consciousness

- **[Palestinian Identity: The Construction of Modern National Consciousness](https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.7312/khal15074)** by Rashid Khalidi - Comprehensive look at development of Palestinian national identity
