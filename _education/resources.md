## Documentaries & Films

**Historical Context:** [Al-Nakba](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLzGHKb8i9vTwtfuXxbuv-rr1z8iUOLuE-) (2008, 4 parts, 1948 from Palestinian perspective), [1948: Creation & Catastrophe](https://www.pbs.org/) (2017, parallel narratives), [The Price of Oslo](https://www.aljazeera.com/program/al-jazeera-world/2013/9/18/the-price-of-oslo) (two-part documentary on Oslo Accords).

**Life Under Occupation:** [Five Broken Cameras](https://www.pbs.org/pov/films/5brokencameras/) (2011, Oscar-nominated chronicle of nonviolent resistance in Bil'in), [Occupation 101](https://occupation101.tv/) (2006, root causes), [Budrus](https://justvision.org/budrus/watch) (community organizer Ayed Morrar uniting Palestinian factions and Israeli supporters).

**Personal Stories:** [Little Palestine: Diary of a Siege](https://www.theguardian.com/film/2023/oct/26/little-palestine-diary-of-a-siege-review-refugee-camp-yarmouk-syria) (2021, life in Yarmouk camp during Syria's civil war), [Gaza Mon Amour](https://tiff.net/events/gaza-mon-amour) (2020, romantic drama in Gaza), [Speed Sisters](https://speedsisters.tv/) (2015, first all-female racing team in Middle East).

**Israeli Perspectives/Critiques:** [The Gatekeepers](https://www.documentary.org/online-feature/secret-sharers-gatekeepers-exposes-hidden-history) (2012, former heads of Shin Bet reflect), [Foxtrot](https://www.sonyclassics.com/foxtrot/) (2017, family tragedy critiquing Israeli military).

**Screen on Campus:** [Film screening toolkit](/campus-organizing/weekly-actions#film-screenings)

## Books

**History:** [The Hundred Years' War on Palestine](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781627798556/thehundredyearswaronpalestine) by Rashid Khalidi (comprehensive history through personal lens), [The Ethnic Cleansing of Palestine](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Ethnic-Cleansing-of-Palestine/Ilan-Pappe/9781851685554) by Ilan Pappé (systematic expulsion in 1948), [Palestine: A Four Thousand Year History](https://www.bloomsbury.com/ca/palestine-9780755649426/) by Nur Masalha (long view of Palestinian identity), [The Question of Palestine](https://typebooks.ca/products/9780679739883) by Edward W. Said (seminal analysis), [Righteous Victims](https://www.palestine-studies.org/en/node/41025) by Benny Morris.

**International Law:** [The Case for Palestine](https://www.dukeupress.edu/the-case-for-palestine) by John Quigley, [Justice for Some](https://www.versobooks.com/) by Noura Erakat (essential legal analysis).

**Personal Narratives:** [Gaza Writes Back](https://justworldbooks.com/books-by-title/gaza-writes-back/) edited by Refaat Alareer (short stories by young writers from Gaza), [After the Last Sky](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/after-the-last-sky/9780231114493) by Edward Said (essays with photos), [The Way to the Spring](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/315663/the-way-to-the-spring-by-ben-ehrenreich/) by Ben Ehrenreich (life in occupied territories), [My Happiness Bears No Relation to Happiness](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300164275/my-happiness-bears-no-relation-to-happiness/) by Adina Hoffman (poet Taha Muhammad Ali), [From Coexistence to Conquest](https://www.amazon.com/Coexistence-Conquest-International-Arab-Israeli-1891-1949/dp/0745325785) by Victor Kattan.

**Graphic Novels:** [Footnotes in Gaza](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9780805092776/footnotesingaza) by Joe Sacco (investigative journalism on 1956 massacres), [Palestine](https://www.fantagraphics.com/products/palestine) by Joe Sacco (classic graphic journalism).

**Poetry:** [Mural](https://www.versobooks.com/en-ca/products/2101-mural) by Mahmoud Darwish (exile, identity, resistance), works by Rafeef Ziadah and Remi Kanazi.

**Identity:** [Palestinian Identity: The Construction of Modern National Consciousness](https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.7312/khal15074) by Rashid Khalidi.

**Reading Lists:** [Decolonize Palestine](https://decolonizepalestine.com/reading-list/), [Haymarket Books - Palestine](https://www.haymarketbooks.org/)
